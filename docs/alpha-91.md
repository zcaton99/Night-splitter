# Alpha 91 — Fox Rasengan progression

Hold Z in human or cloak form to build Rasengan; release Z to thrust it forward. Full Nine-Tails keeps Claw / Fox Fire.

- Tier 1: small blue orb charging in the main hand; 22 damage at full charge, 6-block reach.
- Tier 2: giant blue orb above a raised arm; 44 damage, 10-block reach and 3-block impact radius.
- Tier 3: planetary Rasengan with four smaller orbiting spheres; 70 base damage, 16-block reach and 6-block impact radius. Each mastered element adds its own visible interior and 12% damage. All four add a gold Harmony ring, another 60% damage multiplier and an 8-block radius.
- Full charge takes 1 / 2 / 3 seconds; release earlier for reduced damage. Cooldowns: 4 / 6 / 9 seconds.
- Charges track movement, turn with the hand and clear on cancellation, selection changes, suppression, death or disconnect. Strikes land after a short thrust, respect walls and allies, and finish when switching to elements. No terrain destruction.
- Luffy, Time, Axolotl and the full-Fox reveal/head camera retain their existing behavior.
