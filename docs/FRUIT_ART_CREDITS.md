# Fruit item artwork — alpha.42

The seven fruit textures are adapted from the user-selected One Piece Devil Fruits reference site, https://onepiecedevilfruits.com/. Artwork is credited to its respective creators and rights holders; the code license does not claim ownership of third-party artwork.

The original images are transparent WebP files served by the site's image host. Import preparation converts them to RGBA PNG, removes small disconnected alpha artifacts, trims transparent margins and fits the artwork into 128×128 item textures. The source designs and colors are preserved.

Each fruit has its own Minecraft item model in inventory, in hand and as a dropped item. Existing marked fruit stacks are refreshed on join, periodically in player inventories/open containers, and when dropped item entities load. Ordinary chorus fruit is unchanged. Dark's captured/materialized fruits keep the matching appearance and retain capture tokens and mastery metadata. No abilities or controls are changed by this artwork update.
