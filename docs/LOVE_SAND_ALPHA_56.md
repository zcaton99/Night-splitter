# Love/Sand alpha.56

Love buildup increased: Kiss 40/45/55, Beam 70/85/100, Kick 45/55/70, Arrows 25/30/40. Healthy ordinary zombies now petrify before Kiss damage kills them at every tier. Mob statues last 3/4/5 seconds, leaving time to react and hold Stone Breaker. Player freeze stays capped at two seconds, with its existing recovery window. Caster receives a finisher prompt on full petrification.

Mastered Sand Coffin reach increased from 6 to 8 blocks. Quicksand uses three moving spiral sand bands and loose grains instead of rings. Sables carries enemies along with the vortex and pulls them toward its center, with collision-respecting movement. Sand Drift remains unchanged. Desert Spada pierces multiple enemies along its path, hitting each once; walls still stop it.

Regression now includes real ordinary 20-health zombies at every tier, reaction delay and actual one-second press/release Stone Breaker kills, three enemies pierced by one blade, long Sables carry and an extended-range coffin. All previous player limits, interference, healing, movement, ownership and cleanup checks remain.
