# God Thread environmental charge — alpha.45

Hold Z inside your mastered Birdcage: up to 24 bundles (six animated strands each) rise from actual ground, rooftops and walls around your aimed enemy/point. Bundles grow during the two-second charge and writhe while held. Release at full charge to fire the gathered bundles toward the aimed target. A large aim change rebuilds the field at the new spot.

Charge is visual. Released bundles share six damage groups, preserving the prior maximum of 24 health per target before Harmony. Blocks remain intact. Release, cancel, lost input, cage removal, water, nullification, death and logout remove charge displays. The number of displays is bounded; holding reuses them.

Server regression covers dense real-surface gathering, no charge damage, bounded reuse, release cleanup, actual damage limits and cancel cleanup. Client smoke submits both animated charge and launch bundles. Existing slash, possession, Knight, Glide and Overheat behavior is preserved.
