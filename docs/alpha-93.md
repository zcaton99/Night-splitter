# Alpha 93 — Nine-Tails exit camera and moving Luffy wind-up

- Save the player’s camera before the full-Fox reveal selects third person. Exiting Nine-Tails restores the camera used before transformation; the wide reveal and head zoom remain.
- Held Luffy charges use four attached arm/fist displays, updated with the player’s current position and aim, instead of leaving short-lived arms at previous world positions.
- Red Rocket and Kong Gun’s six-tick retraction also follows the player, then fires from the current hand position.
- Rear wind-up geometry is hidden only from its owner’s first-person view, keeping it clear under network delay. Other players and third-person views retain the animation. Forward punches remain visible.
- Punch damage, reach and cooldowns, approved Fox combat balance, Rasengan and Time remain unchanged.
