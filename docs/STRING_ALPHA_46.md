# String environmental charge and Birdcage close — alpha.46

Includes alpha.45's environmental God Thread charge: up to 24 six-strand bundles rise and writhe from real surfaces, then fire from raised tips when released after two seconds. Release damage remains bounded to 24 health per victim before Harmony.

Hold C again to close Birdcage. Over ten seconds its physical boundary contracts to 50 blocks across and pulls occupants inward with Minecraft collision, including along and off rooftops. Solid obstacles are respected; small step-up/sideways moves can work around obstructions, but a fully sealed structure can resist the pull. No blocks are removed and the pull deals no crushing damage. The cage then converges over one second, releases occupants, and a single white thread rises for two seconds before disappearing.

Regression adds actual rooftop movement, final radius, terrain/health preservation and merge/rise cleanup, alongside environmental charge, possession, attacks, glide and the full existing suite. Client smoke covers the animated bundle render paths. Human gameplay feel and two-client PvP require play testing.
