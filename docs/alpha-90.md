# Creeper Born alpha.90

Fox-only follow-up. Luffy, Time, Axolotl and the animated Fox cloaks retain their alpha.89 behavior.

- Full Nine-Tails holds a wide full-body reveal for 18 ticks, then eases into the skull camera over 26 ticks. First-person aiming begins at 44 ticks (about 2.2 seconds). A near-camera body hide avoids looking through the skull during the last part of the zoom; F5 inspection remains available afterward.
- Claw effects spawn 3.2 blocks ahead of the head viewpoint, align with aim pitch/yaw and follow the owner on the client as well as the server. A red/white moving slash replaces the stationary marks. The damage cone and cooldown remain unchanged.
- Validation covers the reveal timeline, both camera phases in native multiplayer, a post-reveal claw attack and captured game frames.
