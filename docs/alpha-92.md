# Alpha 92 — Golden Cloak defense and independent shadow clones

- Golden Cloak reduces melee/projectile damage by 50% at tier 2 and 60% at mastery (previously 40%). Other damage types, full Fox defenses and Axolotl retain their existing values. The bonus persists while flying or using elements.
- Shadow clones choose separate visible enemies before sharing a target. Half keep ranged spacing while the others close for melee. Mobs can be acquired around the owner; PvP targets still require the owner's forward aim and respect teams.
- Each clone can throw a mini Beast Bomb: 6 / 10 / 14 base damage, with a 5 / 4 / 3 second cooldown. Bombs travel from the clone, collide with walls and targets, and cause no terrain damage. One active bomb per clone and the existing 12-projectile owner limit bound the load.
- Clone expiry, full-Fox transformation, suppression, death and disconnect remove the squad and its outgoing bombs. Shadow Swap and 20-second squad duration remain.
- Full Nine-Tails already grants Beast Bombs 1.6x damage; a fully charged mastered bomb has 102.4 base damage and 10-block radius, versus 64 and 5 in cloak form. This bonus is preserved.
- Rasengan, Luffy, Time and approved cameras are unchanged.
